Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This site is designed to collect as little information as possible.

Nothing directly. The site has no forms, no accounts, no comments and no newsletter sign-up. We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell personal data. If you email us, we use your email address only to reply.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. GitHub may collect technical information such as your IP address when you visit, for security and to run the service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Affiliate links and cookies

This site itself does not set cookies. When you click an affiliate link, you leave this site, and the retailer or affiliate network (for example Awin or CJ) may set cookies so a purchase can be credited to us. Their own privacy and cookie policies apply on their websites.

Changes

If we change how the site handles data (for example by adding privacy-friendly analytics), we will update this page before the change takes effect.
