About Pickwise Notes

Pickwise Notes is a small, independent site with buying guides and comparisons for everyday home and lifestyle purchases, written for readers in English-speaking countries.

How we research

  • We start from what buyers need to decide: size, materials, running costs, warranty, ease of use.
  • We compare products using manufacturer specifications, retailer listings, published standards and independent sources, and we link to the sources we use.
  • Research and drafting are assisted by AI tools. Facts, prices and specifications are checked against the original sources before a guide is published.
  • We update or remove guides when the information they rely on changes.

Tested or researched? We always say which

Most of our guides are based on research, not hands-on testing. Every guide says clearly which products, if any, we tested ourselves. If a guide does not say a product was tested, it was not.

What we will never do

  • Write fake reviews, invent experiences, or claim testing that didn't happen.
  • Publish made-up testimonials, ratings, traffic numbers or "as seen in" claims.
  • Rank a product higher because it pays a higher commission.
  • Accept payment from a brand in exchange for a recommendation.

Spotted a mistake? Please tell us; corrections are welcome.
